Features
Everything you need in one report
📊

Multi-platform analytics

Pull data from Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and YouTube in a single run. No manual exports, no copy-pasting between tools.

📄

Branded PDF + PPTX report

Every report is generated as a polished, client-ready PDF and PowerPoint file with your client's logo and brand colours.

📅

Month-over-month comparisons

Automatically calculates the change from the previous period for every key metric — reach, views, followers, engagement.

📈

Yearly overview charts

13-month follower growth and reach history charts give your clients a clear picture of long-term performance trends.

🏆

Top content spotlight

Automatically identifies the top-performing post, reel, TikTok, and YouTube video for the period with full metrics.

🌍

Multi-language support

Generate reports in English or German. More languages can be added by extending the translation object.

How it works
Connect once, report every month
1

Connect your accounts

Authorise biskwidaDev to read your TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube data via official OAuth flows.

2

Set your date range

Pick a start and end date for the reporting period. The tool handles all API calls automatically.

3

Generate your report

Run a single command and receive a branded PDF and PowerPoint report in seconds.

4

Share with your client

Send the polished PDF directly to your client. Everything is formatted, labelled, and ready to present.
